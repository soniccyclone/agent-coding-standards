description: The book-length development of Date and Darwen's "Third Manifesto," a formal proposal for how a genuinely relational database language should treat types (including user-defined types) without collapsing into either the SQL model or ad hoc object-relational bolt-ons. It works through the consequences in detail — a full type system, inheritance, and the Tutorial D language used as a vehicle for the ideas — arguing throughout that most "object-relational" designs of the era got the integration wrong. Grew out of a shorter 1995 SIGMOD Record paper of the same core argument.
