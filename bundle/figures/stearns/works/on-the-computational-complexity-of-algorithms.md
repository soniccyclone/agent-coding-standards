description: With Juris Hartmanis, introduces time and space as formal cost measures for a Turing machine, defined as functions of input length, and uses them to launch computational complexity as a discipline in its own right. Proves the time and space hierarchy theorems, showing that giving a machine strictly more of either resource strictly increases what it can compute. The resource-bounded machine framework this paper sets up is the direct ancestor of essentially every complexity class defined since, including P and NP.
