description: The 1975 JACM paper that redefines program-size complexity using self-delimiting (prefix-free) programs, which makes the resulting measure obey the same subadditivity and conservation laws as Shannon entropy. Effectively the paper that turns program-size complexity into a proper information theory rather than just a counting exercise.
