# On the Length of Programs for Computing Finite Binary Sequences

**Venue/year:** Journal of the ACM 13(4), 1966, pp. 547-569 (received October 1965, revised March 1966). Phase 4 correction: this file previously recorded JACM 16(1), 1969, pp. 145-159, which is the *sequel* — "On the Length of Programs for Computing Finite Binary Sequences: Statistical Considerations." The PDF at the URL below is the 1966 original, and Chaitin's own timeline in his Recollections confirms part one 1966, part two 1969.
**Source:** https://web.archive.org/web/20131216022500/http://www.cs.auckland.ac.nz:80/CDMTCS/chaitin/acm66.pdf — Wayback Machine snapshot (Dec 2013) of Chaitin's self-archived copy at the University of Auckland CDMTCS site; the live CDMTCS page is now dead (404), so this is a Wayback fallback of a self-archived original. JACM paywalls the publisher version.
