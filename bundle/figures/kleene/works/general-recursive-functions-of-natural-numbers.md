description: Develops the formal theory of general recursive functions, the schema Gödel had sketched (crediting Herbrand) in his 1934 Princeton lectures but left largely unexplored. Kleene works out the class's closure properties and basic representability results, turning a loose sketch into a workable formalism robust enough to serve as a candidate definition of "effectively calculable." This paper and its companion on lambda-definability, published the same year, are the two technical papers behind Kleene's equivalence result underpinning the Church-Turing thesis.
