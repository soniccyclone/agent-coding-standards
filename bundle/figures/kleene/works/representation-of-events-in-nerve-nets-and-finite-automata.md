description: Analyzes what temporal patterns of input ("events") a McCulloch-Pitts finite automaton can recognize, and introduces a notation for combining events by union, concatenation, and iteration to describe them symbolically. Kleene proves that a set of events is representable by such a notation exactly when some finite automaton accepts it, which is the theorem underlying the later equivalence of regular expressions and finite-state recognizers. The notation he defines here is the direct ancestor of what became known as regular expressions.
