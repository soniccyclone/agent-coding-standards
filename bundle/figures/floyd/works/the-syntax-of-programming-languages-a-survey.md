description: A survey of the state of the art in specifying programming-language syntax with formal grammars, mostly variants of phrase-structure grammar, and in mechanizing syntactic analysis from those grammars. Floyd catalogs the approaches in use as of the mid-1960s and identifies open problems in making parsers efficient in time and space and in finding grammar formalisms adequate to real languages. It is a snapshot of the field just before precedence and LR parsing techniques (including Floyd's own) matured it substantially.
