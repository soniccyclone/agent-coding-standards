description: Distills earlier, more elaborate impossibility results for Byzantine agreement, weak agreement, firing squad, and approximate agreement into a single reusable proof technique. Shows that in a network with m faulty nodes, none of these problems is solvable below a threshold on node count (3m+1) or connectivity (2m+1), and that the same indistinguishability argument drives all of them. Functions as the pedagogical companion to FLP — it turns a family of separately-proven results into one general recipe for proving distributed problems unsolvable.
