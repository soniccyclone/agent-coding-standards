description: Church grafts Russell and Ramsey's simple theory of types onto the lambda calculus, producing what is now called simply-typed lambda calculus. This paper is the direct ancestor of type systems in programming language design — the Curry-Howard correspondence and every statically typed functional language trace their type discipline back to this formulation.
