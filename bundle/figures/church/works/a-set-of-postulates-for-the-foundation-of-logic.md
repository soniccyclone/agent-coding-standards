description: The second of a two-part paper (the first appeared in Annals of Mathematics vol. 33, 1932) laying out a formal system for logic built on postulates deliberately structured to avoid the paradoxes that had plagued naive set theory and Russell's type theory. This is Church's pre-lambda-calculus attempt at a minimal logical foundation, and the system here was later shown (by Kleene and Rosser) to itself be inconsistent — a result that pushed Church toward isolating the pure lambda calculus as a separate, more restrained object of study. It's included as the direct intellectual predecessor to the 1941 monograph.
