description: Kolmogorov's short monograph puts probability theory on a rigorous axiomatic footing by identifying it with measure theory - a probability space is a set of outcomes, a sigma-algebra of measurable events over it, and a countably additive measure assigning each event a value between 0 and 1. Before this, probability was argued about informally in terms of frequencies and symmetry, with no agreed formal foundation comparable to what Hilbert-style axiomatization had already given geometry and arithmetic.
