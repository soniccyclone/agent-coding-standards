# Volker Strassen

**Dates:** b. 1936. German mathematician, University of Konstanz (previously Zurich).

## Why a candidate
Proved that naive O(n³) matrix multiplication is not optimal — the archetypal result showing algorithmic technique can beat the "obvious" cost bound, launching the entire field of fast matrix multiplication complexity.

## Top 10 most influential works
Influence concentrates heavily in 2-3 works:
1. "Gaussian Elimination is Not Optimal" (1969, Numerische Mathematik, Strassen's algorithm) — `paywalled`
2. "A Fast Monte-Carlo Test for Primality" (1977, with Solovay, Solovay-Strassen test) — `paywalled`
3. "Relative Bilinear Complexity and Matrix Multiplication" (1987) — `paywalled`
4. "The Asymptotic Spectrum of Tensors and the Exponent of Matrix Multiplication" (1988, FOCS) — `uncertain`
5. "Evaluation of Rational Functions" (1973) — `uncertain`
