description: Codd's attempt to patch what he saw as the original relational model's biggest weakness — it captures data but not the meaning behind it, like which entities are subtypes of others or what a foreign-key-style reference is actually for. Known as RM/T, it adds surrogate keys, entity subtyping, and new operators for these richer relationships on top of the base algebra. It's a direct ancestor of later semantic and object-relational modeling work, though the extensions never displaced the simpler original model in practice.
