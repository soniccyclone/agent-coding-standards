description: Edmonds gives a polynomial-time algorithm for finding a minimum- (or maximum-) weight spanning arborescence rooted at a given vertex in a directed graph — the directed-graph analogue of a minimum spanning tree, which does not reduce to the undirected case. The construction repeatedly identifies and contracts cycles that a naive greedy edge-selection would otherwise create, then expands them back out once the optimal structure is known. It is one of the earliest examples of the "efficient algorithm" standard from "Paths, Trees, and Flowers" applied to a different graph-optimization problem.
