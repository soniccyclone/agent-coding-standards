description: Proposes the "Advice Taker," a hypothetical program that would represent facts about the world as formal statements (largely predicate calculus) and derive new conclusions from them by general-purpose deduction rather than by following problem-specific procedures. It's an early, explicit argument for declarative knowledge representation over procedural encoding, with the deduction mechanism kept separate from the facts being reasoned over. The paper is usually credited as the opening statement of logic-based AI and a forerunner of logic programming.
