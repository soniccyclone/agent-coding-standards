description: Establishes that "acyclic" database schemes — schemes whose relation-scheme hypergraph has no cycle in a specific graph-theoretic sense — are exactly the schemes for which a long list of independently studied, practically important properties all hold simultaneously, including that every pairwise-consistent database is globally consistent, that a join dependency reduces to ordinary multivalued dependencies, and that a full semijoin reducer exists. It ties these previously separate results from database theory and query-processing research together into one equivalence theorem and supplies a simple polynomial-time test (Graham's algorithm) for the property.
