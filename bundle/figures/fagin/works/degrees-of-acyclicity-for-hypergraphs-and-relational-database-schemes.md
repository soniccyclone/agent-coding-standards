description: Shows that the single notion of "acyclic" database scheme from prior work actually splits into a hierarchy of distinct, non-equivalent acyclicity conditions on the underlying hypergraph (alpha-, beta-, gamma-, and Berge-acyclicity), each exactly characterizing a different desirable database property such as unique attribute relationships, monotone join evaluation, or loop-free Bachman diagrams. Proves the equivalences within each degree and gives polynomial-time recognition algorithms for all four degrees. The paper's own running example shows how simply renaming a repeated attribute can upgrade a schema from merely acyclic to the strongest (gamma-acyclic) degree, turning an abstract graph property into a concrete design lever.
