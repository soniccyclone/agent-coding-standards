description: Introduces the multivalued dependency as a generalization of Codd's functional dependency, capturing the case where a set of attribute values is determined independently of the rest of a relation's columns. Uses it to define fourth normal form (4NF), strictly stronger than Boyce-Codd normal form, and proves that any relation schema can be losslessly decomposed into a 4NF family. The formal proofs and worked university-database example ground the new dependency concept directly in decomposition theory rather than ad hoc design rules.
