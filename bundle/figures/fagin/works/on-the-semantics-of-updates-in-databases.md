description: Treats a database as a logical theory and asks what it should mean to insert or delete a sentence when the theory has integrity constraints or derived facts that make the "obvious" edit ambiguous or inconsistent. Proposes resolving an update by picking the revised theory (or theories) that changes the existing facts as little as possible, with deletions minimized ahead of insertions, and extends this to prioritized "logical databases" so that hand-authored constraints can outrank ordinary stored facts during that minimization. Also reframes the long-standing view-update problem as a special case: a user's requested change to a view is treated as new information to translate into the base theory, rather than as an edit whose "correct" database-level effect must be reverse-engineered.
