description: Defines domain-key normal form (DK/NF), the first normal form stated purely in terms of the primitive notions of attribute domains and keys rather than in terms of functional, multivalued, or join dependencies. Gives formal definitions of insertion and deletion anomaly and proves a schema is in DK/NF exactly when it has neither, which lets a designer judge "goodness" of a schema without reasoning about any specific dependency type at all. Also shows how bounded (finite) domain sizes interact combinatorially with the classical normal forms, a factor earlier normal-form definitions had implicitly ignored.
