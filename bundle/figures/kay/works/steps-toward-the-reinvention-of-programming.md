description: A Viewpoints Research Institute technical report to the National Science Foundation describing the STEPS project's attempt to build a complete personal-computing system — networking, graphics, GUI, and applications — in roughly 20,000 lines of code by inventing small domain-specific languages for each layer instead of writing general-purpose code throughout. It is a direct continuation of Kay's long-standing argument that most software complexity is self-inflicted and that the right meta-languages can collapse systems by orders of magnitude. The report documents both the approach, including the OMeta language-workbench tooling, and early working results.
