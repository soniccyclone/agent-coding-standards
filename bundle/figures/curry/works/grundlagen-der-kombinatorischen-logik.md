It independently re-derives and formalizes combinatory logic, the system Schönfinkel had sketched a few years earlier, building a calculus of combinators that expresses functional application and composition without named or bound variables. Part I lays out the general foundations and basic combinators; Part II works through further consequences and formal properties, together establishing the systematic apparatus later combinatory-logic and lambda-calculus work builds on.
