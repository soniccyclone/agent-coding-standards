description: Proposes a risk-driven process model where a project cycles repeatedly through planning, risk analysis, prototyping/development, and review rather than moving through fixed phases once. Each pass around the spiral is sized by how much risk remains, so cheap, uncertain projects can loop fast and expensive, uncertain ones get more scrutiny before commitment. Boehm frames it explicitly as a generalization that can subsume waterfall, incremental, and prototyping approaches as special cases.
