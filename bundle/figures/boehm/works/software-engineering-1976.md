Boehm uses cost-distribution data from real projects to argue that later-lifecycle defects are dramatically more expensive to fix than early ones, an observation that underpins most of his later work on process and estimation. Reads as a snapshot of the field's growing pains right as it was trying to become an engineering discipline rather than a craft.
