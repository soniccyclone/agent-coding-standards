description: Makes the case that the general-purpose relational engine, tuned to serve every workload from OLTP to data warehousing to streaming, is no longer the right default and that the market will instead fracture into specialized engines optimized per workload. Stonebraker and Cetintemel back the claim with performance comparisons showing purpose-built systems (column stores, stream processors, text search engines) beating general RDBMSs by an order of magnitude or more on their respective workloads. The paper is effectively the manifesto behind Stonebraker's subsequent run of specialized-engine startups (Vertica, StreamBase, VoltDB, and others).
