description: Backus's 1959 conference paper describes a formal metanotation for specifying the grammar of the algebraic language that became ALGOL 60, using recursive production rules to define legal programs precisely instead of relying on prose description. Peter Naur adapted and extended this notation for the ALGOL 60 report, and it became known as Backus-Naur Form. The paper is the origin point for treating a programming language's syntax as a formal object subject to its own notation, rather than an informal convention.
