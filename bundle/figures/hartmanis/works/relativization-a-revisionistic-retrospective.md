It revisits two decades of results built on that idea, questions how historically accurate the usual telling of the story is, and weighs whether relativization's dominance was already ending in light of newer non-relativizing results about interactive protocols. This is Hartmanis's own contribution to the relativized-complexity line of work the original figure stub attributed (uncertainly, and it turns out incorrectly) to Baker and Gill directly.
