description: The overview paper for the whole Plan 9 operating system, explaining why Bell Labs built a new distributed OS rather than patch Unix onto a network of cheap microcomputers. It lays out the three design principles the system commits to: everything is named and accessed as a file, a single protocol (9P) reaches all of them whether local or remote, and per-process namespaces let disjoint services be joined into one private hierarchy. The paper is the clearest single statement of how Plan 9 generalizes and hardens Unix's "everything is a file" idea instead of discarding it.
