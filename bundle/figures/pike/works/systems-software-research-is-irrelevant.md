description: A polemic talk arguing that academic systems research had, by 2000, become disconnected from where real systems problems live — Pike contends most published OS research recycles minor variations on solved problems while industry and a shrinking set of research labs (his own included) do the work that actually changes how systems are built. It's less a technical paper than a diagnosis of incentive structures in CS research, delivered by someone who had just helped build an entire alternative operating system (Plan 9) that industry mostly ignored. Widely cited since as a touchstone for debates about research relevance and insularity in systems academia.
