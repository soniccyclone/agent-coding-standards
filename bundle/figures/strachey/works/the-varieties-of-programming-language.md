description: A comparative survey of the "domains" of values a language can manipulate — L-values, R-values, procedures, labels, and so on — used as a framework for classifying and comparing languages by which domains they support and how they let those domains combine. It's a direct continuation of the taxonomy work started in "Fundamental Concepts."
