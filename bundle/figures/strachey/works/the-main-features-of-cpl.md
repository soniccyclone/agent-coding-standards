description: An informal introduction to CPL (Combined Programming Language), a joint Cambridge/London project extending Algol 60 with richer data description, a unified expression/command structure, and facilities for manipulating non-numerical data. Strachey is a co-author alongside the language's other Cambridge and London designers.
