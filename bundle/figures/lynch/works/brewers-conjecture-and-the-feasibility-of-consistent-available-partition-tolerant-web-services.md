description: Gives the first formal proof of what became known as the CAP theorem — that a distributed web service cannot simultaneously guarantee strong consistency, availability, and tolerance of network partitions. Proves the impossibility in the asynchronous network model, then discusses weaker guarantees (partial synchrony, relaxed consistency) that let real systems trade one property off deliberately. The paper that turned Eric Brewer's 2000 keynote conjecture into a citable theorem, and set the vocabulary ("CAP") the NoSQL/distributed-database era argued in.
