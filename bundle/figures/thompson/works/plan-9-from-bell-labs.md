description: The full system overview of Plan 9, the distributed successor to Unix built by much of the original Unix team. It describes assembling a computing environment out of separate CPU servers, file servers, and terminals joined only by a single file-oriented protocol (9P) and per-process name spaces, replacing the growing pile of Unix special cases (sockets, ioctls, windowing hacks) with one uniform mechanism applied consistently. The paper is the clearest statement of what the Unix team concluded Unix itself had gotten wrong by the mid-1980s and how they proposed to fix it.
