description: A wide-ranging survey, written for a general mathematical audience, that consolidates Post's results on recursively enumerable sets and pushes them further — introducing what became known as "creative sets" and laying out a research program of open problems about degrees of unsolvability. One of those open problems, whether there exists an r.e. set of intermediate degree strictly between the solvable sets and the halting problem, stood for over a decade as "Post's problem" until Friedberg and Muchnik independently resolved it with the priority method. The paper also works to make Gödel-style incompleteness and undecidability legible outside the small circle already fluent in the formalism.
