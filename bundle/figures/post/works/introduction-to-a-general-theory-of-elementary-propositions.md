description: Post's doctoral dissertation work, in which he proves that the propositional-logic fragment of Whitehead and Russell's Principia Mathematica is both consistent and complete, and formalizes truth tables as a systematic mechanical decision procedure for propositional validity — developed independently of and around the same time as Wittgenstein's similar device in the Tractatus. It also proves a completeness result for a generalized many-valued logic Post constructs along the way. The paper is an early, clean instance of treating a formal system itself as an object of mathematical study from outside that system, the same move that Gödel's incompleteness results would later depend on.
