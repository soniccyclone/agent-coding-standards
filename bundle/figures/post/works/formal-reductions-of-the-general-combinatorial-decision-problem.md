description: Develops Post's "canonical system" formalism — production rules that rewrite strings under simple pattern-matching conditions — as a normal form that other combinatorial decision problems can be mechanically reduced into. Post shows that a wide class of such problems can be brought into a single canonical shape, "normal systems," making that shape the natural target for later unsolvability proofs rather than having to re-derive machinery from scratch for each new problem. It's the detailed working-out of the string-rewriting apparatus that Post's later, shorter unsolvability papers lean on without re-deriving.
