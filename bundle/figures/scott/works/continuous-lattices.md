description: Introduces continuous lattices as a class of topological spaces with a strong extension property, then proves the result that made a rigorous model of the untyped lambda calculus possible: every continuous lattice can be embedded in one that is homeomorphic to its own function space. That self-application construction is the mathematical core underneath domain theory's account of recursive types and higher-order functions.
