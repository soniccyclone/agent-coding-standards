description: Scott's first public statement of what became domain theory, proposing that data types be modeled as complete lattices ordered by an "approximation" relation rather than as bare sets. This ordering lets recursively defined functions be understood as limits of successive approximations, giving a rigorous account of what a recursive definition actually denotes. The paper predates the more polished 1976 treatment and shows the theory still being worked out in real time.
