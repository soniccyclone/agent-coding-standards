description: A detailed hardware and logical design for a stored-program electronic computer, submitted to the National Physical Laboratory, covering memory ("storages"), arithmetic circuitry, instruction format, and an estimate of cost and build time. It is one of the first complete engineering specifications for what would now be recognized as a general-purpose computer, written by someone who had already worked out the abstract theory of computation and was now mapping it onto vacuum-tube hardware. The machine as specified is markedly more minimal and instruction-economical than contemporaries like the EDVAC design.
