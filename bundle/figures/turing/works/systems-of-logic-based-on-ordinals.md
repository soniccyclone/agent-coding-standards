description: Turing's Princeton PhD thesis, working out how to escape Godel's incompleteness results for a fixed formal system by repeatedly adjoining true-but-unprovable statements (like the system's own consistency) and iterating the process along a transfinite sequence of ordinals. Along the way it introduces the oracle machine, a Turing machine augmented with an idealized black-box answer source for some non-computable problem, opening the study of relative computability. It is denser and less cited than the 1936 paper but is where several ideas complexity theory later formalized first show up.
