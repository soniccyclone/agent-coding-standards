description: Proposes replacing the question "can machines think?" with a concrete behavioral test, the imitation game, in which a human judge tries to distinguish a machine from a person through text conversation alone. Turing works through the leading objections of his day (theological, mathematical, the argument from consciousness) and dismisses each in turn, arguing intelligence should be judged by external behavior rather than by an unobservable internal state. It reframed machine intelligence as an engineering and definitional problem rather than a metaphysical one, and gave the field its most durable informal benchmark.
