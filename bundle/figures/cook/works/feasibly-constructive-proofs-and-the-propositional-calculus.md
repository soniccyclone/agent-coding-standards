description: Defines the equational theory PV ("polynomial-time verifiable"), a system intended to capture exactly the reasoning that can be carried out about polynomial-time-computable functions using only feasible methods. Cook connects the existence of short propositional proofs of tautologies to whether NP is closed under complementation, linking proof length directly to the P-vs-NP question. This paper opened proof complexity as a research area by treating the length of a proof, not just its existence, as a formal complexity measure.
