description: Cox argues that software will only escape its productivity crisis the way manufacturing did — not through better methodology or languages, but by shifting from process-centered craft production to a marketplace of purchasable, off-the-shelf components. He frames object technology as the mechanism for that shift, contrasting the software industry's obsession with process (languages, methodologies) against mature industries that standardize on products instead. The piece is the clearest statement of the economic argument behind his "Software-IC" concept.
