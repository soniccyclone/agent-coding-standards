# What if there's a Silver Bullet... And the Competition Gets it First?

**Author(s):** Brad Cox

**Venue/year:** Guest Editorial, Journal of Object-Oriented Programming, June 1992; republished in Dr. Dobb's Journal, October 1992 (per Cox's own 1998 publications list). Note: this is distinct from — though thematically continuous with and textually overlapping — "There is a Silver Bullet" (Byte, October 1990), which is Byte's retitled reprint of "Planning the Software Industrial Revolution."
