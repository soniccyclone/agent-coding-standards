description: Cox popularizes Ryoichi Mori's "superdistribution" scheme for English-speaking readers, proposing that software vendors give programs away for free and collect revenue by metering actual use rather than restricting copying. He works through the mechanics — tamper-resistant usage-tracking hardware, recursive revenue-sharing up a chain of component vendors, and the useright/copyright distinction borrowed from broadcast licensing — as a way to make ease-of-copying an asset for a component marketplace instead of a threat to it. This is the accessible magazine-length statement of the argument Cox later expanded into his 1996 book of nearly the same name.
