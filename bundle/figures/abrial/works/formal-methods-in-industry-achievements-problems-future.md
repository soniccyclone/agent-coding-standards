# Formal Methods in Industry: Achievements, Problems, Future

**Venue/year:** ICSE '06 — Proceedings of the 28th International Conference on Software Engineering, ACM, 2006, pp. 761-768 (invited talk). DOI: 10.1145/1134285.1134406.
**Source:** https://math.pku.edu.cn/teachers/jrabrial/FM-IndApp.pdf — hosted on Peking University's School of Mathematical Sciences server, on the page built for Abrial's 2011 term there as a visiting professor teaching Event-B; listed there by name alongside "Faultless Systems: Yes We Can!" as his own reference reading for the course. Verified as a live, directly downloadable PDF (HTTP 200, application/pdf, 7 pages).
