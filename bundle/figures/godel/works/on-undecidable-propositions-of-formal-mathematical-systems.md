# On Undecidable Propositions of Formal Mathematical Systems

**Author(s):** Notes by S. C. Kleene and J. B. Rosser (of lectures delivered by Gödel).
**Venue/year:** Mimeographed lecture notes, Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton, February-May 1934. Reprinted, with Gödel's corrections and an added Postscriptum, in Martin Davis (ed.), *The Undecidable* (Raven Press, 1965), and in *Kurt Gödel: Collected Works, Vol. I: Publications 1929-1936* (Oxford University Press, 1986), pp. 346-372.
