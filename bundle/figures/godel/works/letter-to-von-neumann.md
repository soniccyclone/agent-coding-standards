# Letter to John von Neumann

**Venue/year:** Personal letter, dated Princeton, 20 March 1956; von Neumann was terminally ill and the letter went unanswered. Rediscovered in von Neumann's papers and first published (in German with English commentary) by Juris Hartmanis in 1989.
