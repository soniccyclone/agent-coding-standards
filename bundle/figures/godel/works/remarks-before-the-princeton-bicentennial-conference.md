description: A short conference remark proposing that Turing's analysis of mechanical computability — which Gödel treats as the first genuinely absolute (representation-independent) definition in this area of logic — should be extended to give equally absolute notions of definability and of provability. Where "computable" had been pinned down independent of any particular formalism, "provable" and "definable" were still relative to whatever system you picked; Gödel is asking whether that relativity can be removed the same way. It's a brief but influential statement of a research program later authors picked up under the label "formalism-freeness."
