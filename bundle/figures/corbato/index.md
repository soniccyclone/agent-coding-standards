# Fernando Corbató

**Dates:** 1926-2019. MIT professor.

## Why a candidate
Organized and led the team that proved interactive multi-user timesharing worked, establishing the OS-as-resource-scheduler model Unix inherited wholesale.

## Top 10 most influential works
1. "Introduction and Overview of the Multics System" (1965, with Vyssotsky) — `public` (self-archived on multicians.org)
2. "Multics: The First Seven Years" (1972, with Clingen, Saltzer) — `public` (multicians.org)
3. "On Building Systems That Will Fail" (1991 Turing lecture) — `uncertain`
4. "A New Remote-Accessed Man-Machine System" (1962, the original CTSS paper) — `uncertain`

4 distinct, verifiable works.
