# An Experimental Time-Sharing System

**Author(s):** F. J. Corbató, Marjorie Merwin Daggett, Robert C. Daley
**Venue/year:** AFIPS Conference Proceedings 21 (1962 Spring Joint Computer Conference), pp. 335-344.
**Source:** http://larch-www.lcs.mit.edu:8001/~corbato/sjcc62/ — self-archived by Corbató on his own MIT LCS homepage, page states "Scanned and transcribed by F. J. Corbató from the original SJCC Paper of May 3, 1962." Confirmed live (HTTP 200) with full paper text.

**Note:** The Phase 1/2 stub listed this work under the title "A New Remote-Accessed Man-Machine System." No paper with that exact title could be found under Corbató's name; this appears to be a mistaken title from the lower-rigor pass. The actual 1962 CTSS launch paper, verified here, is titled "An Experimental Time-Sharing System."
