description: The paper that imported tense logic into computer science, arguing that program correctness — especially for concurrent and nonterminating programs — is a claim about how executions unfold over time, not just about input/output relations. Pnueli adapts a modal logic of time into a formal system for stating and proving properties like invariance and eventuality directly. It founded the temporal-logic approach to specification that model checking later automated, and is the main basis for his 1996 Turing Award.
