description: One of Péter's earliest papers on recursive function theory, showing that several variant recursion schemes then in circulation — including course-of-values recursion — reduce to ordinary primitive recursion and add no new functions beyond it. It is an early move in disentangling which recursion schemes are genuinely stronger than primitive recursion and which just look stronger, a question her later work would turn into a systematic classification. Published under her birth name, R. Politzer, before she adopted the surname Péter.
