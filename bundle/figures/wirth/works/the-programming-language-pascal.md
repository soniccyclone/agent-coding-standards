description: The formal definition of Pascal, describing a small language of strict, statically-checked data types (records, arrays, sets, enumerations, pointers) built as a teaching-oriented successor to Algol 60. Pascal went on to become the dominant teaching language of the following two decades and the direct ancestor of Modula-2 and Oberon.
