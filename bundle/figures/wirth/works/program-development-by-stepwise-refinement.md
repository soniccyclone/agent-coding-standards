description: Walks through the design of a program (a numeric puzzle solver) as a sequence of decreasingly abstract versions, each one committing to a few more concrete decisions about data representation and control structure than the last. Wirth frames every refinement step as a design decision that should be justified and criteria-driven rather than accidental, and argues that decomposition of tasks and decomposition of data have to proceed together. The paper became the canonical reference for top-down structured program design in the 1970s.
