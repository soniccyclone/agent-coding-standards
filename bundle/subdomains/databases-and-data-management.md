# Databases & Data Management

## Definition
The relational model and declarative approaches to data management. Despite
sitting adjacent to systems programming in most curricula, the actual
intellectual lineage here (Codd's relational algebra, declarative query
languages) is notably closer to the Church/primitive-count lineage than to
the implementation-mapping layer.
