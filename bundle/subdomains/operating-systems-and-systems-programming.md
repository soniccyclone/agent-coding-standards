# Operating Systems & Systems Programming

## Definition
Kernels, systems programming, the layer where software meets hardware
mechanism most directly. Structurally the implementation-mapping layer's home
turf — Turing-lineage reasoning about mechanism and state isn't a competing
philosophy here, it's the actual subject matter.
