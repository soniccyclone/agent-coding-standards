# Formal Methods & Verification

## Definition
Where correctness is pursued directly through proof — Hoare logic, weakest
preconditions, model checking, specification languages. Split out from
Foundations of Computation on purpose: it's Church-style formalism pointed
specifically at verifiability rather than at expressiveness or minimality for
its own sake.
