# Distributed Systems & Concurrency

## Definition
Consensus, concurrent correctness, and reasoning under network unreliability
— the subdomain where parallelizability and verifiability have to hold
simultaneously under conditions (partition, delay, partial failure) that
single-machine formalisms don't have to account for.
